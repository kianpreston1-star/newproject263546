# Hook library

The variety injector picks one hook style per video (the workflow sets `{{hook_style}}` to one of
these at random). After you've posted enough to see retention data, prune this list down to your
top 2–3 performers so the machine leans into what works.

| Hook style | Pattern | Example |
|------------|---------|---------|
| `curiosity_gap` | Tease a payoff without revealing it | "This free tool does what Photoshop charges $240/yr for." |
| `contrarian` | State the opposite of common belief | "Stop learning to code. Here's what actually matters now." |
| `direct_promise` | Promise a concrete outcome | "In 30 seconds you'll never forget how RAM works." |
| `number_list` | Lead with a countable payoff | "5 AI tools that feel illegal to know." |
| `mistake_warning` | Warn about a common error | "You're using ChatGPT wrong — here's the fix." |
| `question` | Open with a question the viewer can't answer | "Why does your phone get slower every year?" |
| `story_cold_open` | Drop into a mini-story mid-action | "He automated his whole job and nobody noticed for 6 months." |
| `myth_vs_fact` | Set up a myth to bust | "Myth: AI will take all jobs. The data says something weirder." |
| `before_after` | Contrast two states | "This took editors 4 hours. Now it's one click." |
| `shock_stat` | Lead with a surprising (true) number | "92% of these go viral for one reason." |

## Structures (the injector also rotates these)
- `problem_twist_payoff` — set a problem, add a twist, resolve.
- `list` — numbered points, fast cuts.
- `myth_vs_fact` — claim, correction, why it matters.
- `story` — cold open, escalation, lesson.
- `how_it_works` — question, mechanism, "so that's why".

## Pacing
- `punchy` — short sentences, hard cuts, every ~1.5s a new beat.
- `narrative` — smoother flow, fewer cuts, builds tension.

**Rule for all hooks:** the claim must be true and the video must deliver on it. Hooks that
overpromise tank retention and train the algorithm against you.
