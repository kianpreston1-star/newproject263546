# Niche pack: AI & Technology (example)

Copy this file to make a pack for your own niche, then point the LLM nodes at it (or paste its
contents into the system prompt). This pack overrides the generic defaults with niche-specific
tone, good/bad examples, and guardrails.

## Audience
Curious non-experts and early-adopter tinkerers who want to feel ahead of the curve. They reward
"I didn't know that" and practical tools they can use today.

## Tone
Energetic, plain-English, slightly witty. Explain like a smart friend, not a textbook. No jargon
without an instant plain definition.

## Great angles (lean in)
- A specific free/cheap tool that does something surprising.
- "How X actually works" mechanisms (phones, AI models, chips, the internet).
- Contrarian takes backed by real facts.
- Before/after automation and productivity wins.

## Avoid
- Vague "AI is changing everything" filler.
- Over-covered news everyone's already seen.
- Unverifiable claims, fake benchmarks, invented company quotes.
- Fearmongering ("AI will kill us all") — low retention, bad for monetization.

## Example hooks (good)
- "This free AI tool turns a screenshot into working code."
- "Your Wi-Fi is slow for a reason nobody tells you."
- "Stop paying for Grammarly. This does it free and offline."

## Example hooks (bad — don't)
- "AI is the future!!!" (no payoff)
- "Top 10 AI tools 2026" (generic, over-covered)

## Fact safety
This niche attracts corrections. If unsure of a spec/number/date, keep the claim qualitative
("a lot faster") rather than inventing a figure. Accuracy protects the channel's credibility and
its monetization eligibility.
